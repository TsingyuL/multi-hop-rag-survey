01

Next-Hop Discovery

Map the current evidence state to a concrete acquisition target. Inspect the interface between what is known and what should be sought next.

Evidence-conditioned retrievalGoldEn Retriever · MDR · BaleenDoes accumulated context preserve the bridge needed for the next retrieval?
Explicit decomposition or reasoningDecompRC · Self-Ask · IRCoTDoes the subquestion or reasoning state express a valid next information need?
Missing-information modelingMIGRES · S2G-RAGCan the controller distinguish insufficient evidence from what is specifically missing?
Structured or agentic searchToG · Search-R1 · RAG-GymHow do frontier actions, search rewards, and retrieval budgets shape the next hop?
02

Path Management

Estimate the future value of partial evidence trajectories. A locally weak item may still be necessary for a valid future chain.

Partial-chain retentionPathRetriever · BeamDR · M3Do valid chains survive pruning under a fixed search budget?
Set or subgraph managementCORE · CIRAG · CatRAGIs compatibility among evidence units scored jointly?
Lookahead or tree searchPPRR · STEMDoes the controller estimate future reachability instead of current relevance alone?
03

Evidence Sufficiency

Decide whether the current external evidence state is adequate for the remaining task—separately from deciding what to retrieve next.

Answerability judgmentIDRQA · AISOCan adequacy be diagnosed before final answer generation?
Adaptive stoppingStop-RAG · FrugalRAGMeasure premature stopping, excessive search, and cost calibration.
Gap-aware diagnosisMIGRES · S2G-RAGDoes the controller expose missing evidence instead of only a binary label?
Evidence-aware controlSEMA-RAG · IterCOMPHow do state, compression, and continuation decisions interact?
04

Error Recovery

Require an observable failure signal followed by a corrective state transition. Requesting more evidence alone is not recovery.

Local correctionDr3 · ARI-KBQAIs a specific off-topic or invalid intermediate state identified and corrected?
Rollback or reversible stateReAgent · RetroRAGDo prior evidence and reasoning states remain editable or restorable?
Verifier-guided replanningSR-RAG · D2PlanDoes a verifier localize the failure and constrain the next plan?
05

Evidence Composition

Once required evidence units are available, ask whether the system can bind, compare, aggregate, or transform them correctly.

Graph binding or propagationDFGN · HGN · KIFGraphDo relations and message passing bind the correct evidence units?
Explicit intermediate variablesDecompRC · PathFiD · SSCOTDo intermediate answers or slots expose the join variable?
Join, comparison, or aggregationS3HQAIs the required operator explicit and can its failure be localized?
Heterogeneous alignmentDEHG · HybridQA · MultiModalQAAre table, text, graph, and image evidence aligned before composition?

Evaluation routes

Benchmark reading paths

Choose benchmarks by the evidence dependency and operation they expose, then compare retrieval settings before comparing headline scores.

HotpotQA · two-hop supporting facts

Supporting-fact labels are an imperfect proxy for the complete latent evidence chain.

2WikiMultiHopQA + MuSiQue

Match retrieval settings and distractor construction before comparing scores.

HoVer · multi-hop verification

Fact verification is not directly interchangeable with answer extraction.

QASC · scientific composition

The composed fact can introduce a bridge concept absent from the question.

HybridQA + MultiModalQA

Separate evidence accessibility from the downstream composition operator.

Keep discovery and evidence coding separate

The 771-paper reference library expands discovery, but citation frequency does not establish primary-source review, scope eligibility, or challenge assignment. Use the frozen reviewed evidence map for manuscript-level prevalence and overlap counts.
