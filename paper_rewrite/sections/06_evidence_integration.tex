\section{Evidence Integration}
\label{sec:evidence_integration}

Evidence integration converts the material acquired across hops into a representation that can support an answer or report. The problem is not equivalent to retrieving more documents: the system must determine which evidence is jointly necessary, preserve bridge relations under a context budget, organize sources for a reader or generator, and combine information without losing provenance or introducing unsupported claims.
