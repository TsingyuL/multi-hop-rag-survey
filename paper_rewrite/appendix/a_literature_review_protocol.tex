\section{Literature Review Protocol}
\label{app:review_protocol}

This appendix will report the search sources, query families, date range, deduplication procedure, inclusion and exclusion criteria, primary-category assignment rule, secondary tagging scheme, metadata verification status, and adjudication procedure. The literature artifact distinguishes discovered records, source-identified records, metadata-verified records, and full-text-audited records; these states are not treated as interchangeable evidence of review depth.
