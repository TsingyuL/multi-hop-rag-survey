\section{Diagnostic Audit}
\label{app:diagnostic_audit}

The diagnostic audit uses a stratified 70-paper selection: 15 initial-retrieval papers, 25 follow-up-retrieval papers, 20 evidence-integration papers, and 10 feedback-learning papers. Each record will be coded from the full text for the claimed improvement, task and evidence setting, process metrics, trajectory availability, intervention or ablation evidence, cost reporting, and unresolved evaluation gaps. Missing evidence is recorded as \emph{not reported}, \emph{not applicable}, or \emph{unclear}; these values are not collapsed.
